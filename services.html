Our Auto Glass Services

From rock chip repairs to full windshield replacements and commercial fleet services, we provide top-tier auto glass solutions with a focus on safety and quality.

Windshield Replacement

Your windshield is a critical safety component of your vehicle, providing structural support to the roof and aiding in proper airbag deployment. We use only OEM-equivalent glass and premium urethane adhesives to ensure a factory-safe installation every time.

  • ADAS calibration available
  • Same-day service in most cases

Rock Chip Repair

Don't let a small chip become a large crack. Our advanced resin injection process restores the structural integrity of your windshield and prevents the damage from spreading, often saving you the cost of a full replacement.

  • Takes only 30 minutes
  • Often fully covered by insurance
  • Stops cracks from spreading

Lexan Polycarbonate Sheet

Tough, lightweight, optically clear glazing for equipment, vehicles, and projects. Lexan is far more impact-resistant than glass and weighs about half as much, cut to your size or template.

  • Machine guards and safety shields
  • Equipment, truck, and trailer windows
  • Cut to size or to your pattern

Margard Abrasion-Resistant Sheet

Lexan polycarbonate with a hard coating for surfaces that get scrubbed, wiped, and washed. Margard resists scratching and many harsh cleaners, so it stays clear longer.

  • Ideal for frequently cleaned surfaces

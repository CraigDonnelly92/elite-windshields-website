Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our auto glass services.

Do you handle insurance claims?▼

Yes, we work directly with all major insurance providers and can often bill them directly, making the process seamless for you.

How long does a windshield replacement take?▼

Most replacements are completed in under an hour, with same-day service available in most cases.

Can my rock chip be repaired, or do I need a replacement?▼

Most chips smaller than a quarter and not in the driver's direct line of sight can be repaired. Our technicians will assess and advise the best option.

Yes, mobile service is available throughout our service area.

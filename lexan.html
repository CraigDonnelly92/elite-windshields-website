Lexan Polycarbonate Sheet

Tough, lightweight, optically clear glazing cut to fit your equipment, vehicle, or project.

Strength where glass falls short

Lexan is a polycarbonate sheet known for exceptional impact resistance. Where glass can crack or shatter from flying debris, vibration, or a stray hit, Lexan flexes and holds together — and it weighs roughly half as much.

  • ✓ Far more impact-resistant than glass
  • ✓ About half the weight of glass
  • ✓ Clear optical quality
  • ✓ Cut, drilled, and shaped to your pattern

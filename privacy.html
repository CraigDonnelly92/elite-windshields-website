Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Elite Windshields ("we," "us," or "our"), located in Cleardale, Alberta, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the information you share with us when you request a quote or service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, and the details of your vehicle or equipment and the work you need. We may also collect information about your insurance claim if you ask us to help with one, and any photos or measurements you send us for a quote.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your questions, prepare quotes, schedule and carry out work, handle payment and insurance paperwork, and follow up on the service we provided. We may also use it to meet legal and accounting obligations. With your consent, we may also use your contact information to send you promotions, special offers, and news about our products and services by phone, text message, or email, now or at a later date. You can opt out of promotional messages at any time (see Section 7). We do not use your information for purposes you would not reasonably expect.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to provide our services or when the law requires it. For example, we may share claim details with your insurance provider at your request, or work with suppliers and payment providers to complete your order. Anyone we share information with is expected to protect it.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics of its own. Some content comes from third parties, such as the embedded Google Map, the "Get Directions" link, and links to our Google reviews. These services are run by Google and may collect information, such as your IP address or cookies, under their own privacy policies. This website is hosted by a third-party provider that may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests, for security and operational purposes.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive information, such as payment card numbers, through website forms or email.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any period required for accounting, tax, or legal reasons. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal requirements to keep it. You may also withdraw your consent to our use of your information, although this may limit our ability to provide some services. To stop receiving promotional messages, reply STOP to a text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us directly, and we will stop. To make any other request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's consent.

9. Links to Other Websites

This website may link to other sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the website or our services after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions or requests about your personal information? Contact Elite Windshields at 780-835-1844 or eliteautoglass24@gmail.com.

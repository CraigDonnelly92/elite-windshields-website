Margard Abrasion-Resistant Sheet

Lexan polycarbonate with a hard coating for surfaces that get scrubbed, wiped, and washed.

A tougher surface on tough glazing

Margard starts with the impact strength of polycarbonate and adds a hard coating that stands up to scratching, abrasion, and many harsh cleaners. It stays clear longer in places where plain sheet would haze or scuff.
